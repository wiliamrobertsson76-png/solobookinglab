About SoloBookingLab

Publisher: William Robertsson (individual), Sweden.
Registered: 2026.
Contact: see the contact page.

What this site is

A small, independent resource for solo and mobile service businesses (mobile dog groomers, mobile car detailers, solo cleaners, driving instructors): a rule-based Fit Finder for booking stacks, transparent product tests, and vertical guides. Every volatile fact — prices, plan limits, capabilities — comes from vendor pages or our own documented tests, labeled and date-stamped. What we have not verified, we say.

History: this site previously covered newsletter-platform costs under the name NewsletterStack. That project was archived on 2026-09-05 and the site pivoted; see the corrections log.

What this site is not

  • It is not a company or a media brand. One person runs it.
  • It does not host ads, trackers or cookies of any kind.
  • It does not publish "reviews" of products nobody here has actually used. Where we describe features, they come from vendor documentation or direct testing in free plans, and the page says which.

How it is funded

Some outbound links are affiliate links. If you buy something through one, the vendor pays a commission — never you. Affiliate links are disclosed next to where they appear and marked with rel="sponsored". The funding model never overrides the verification rules: recommendations state their criteria, and the same criteria apply to every platform.

Technical choices, explained

The site is static HTML with one small, auditable script for the calculator. That is why it loads fast, works with JavaScript disabled, and cannot track you. There is nothing to opt out of.
