Booking software for mobile car detailers

A detailing job at a client's driveway is unpredictable: a "maintenance wash" and a corrected paint job are different worlds, weather moves bookings, and you need to know access and water situation before you drive. Here is the stack to demand — and what is (and is not) verified about current tools.

What a mobile detailer should demand from a booking stack

  1. Pre-visit intake with details/photos: vehicle, condition, access, water/power availability at the location.
  2. Variable service lengths — quote-to-time mismatch destroys schedules.
  3. Deposits to protect long slots, with a rescheduling path when weather intervenes.
  4. Reminders that reduce the "I forgot they were coming today" cancel.
  5. Service areas / distance rules so far-away jobs cost what they cost.
  6. Cancellation policy you control (cutoffs, weather exceptions).
  7. Data export — client and vehicle history must leave with you.
