Privacy

Last updated: 2026-09-04

The short version

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no third-party scripts and collects no personal data. There is nothing to consent to and nothing to opt out of.

What happens when you visit

The site is served as static files by the hosting provider (currently GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub Inc.). Like any web server, it processes standard technical request data (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) for security and delivery. That processing is governed by the host's own terms and privacy policy, not by this site.

Affiliate links

When you click an outbound affiliate link, the destination vendor (and its affiliate tracking partner) may set their own cookies on their domains and process data under their own privacy policies. That happens on their sites, after you leave ours, and only if you click such a link. Affiliate links on this site are disclosed where they appear.

Children

The site is intended for a general adult audience and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes — for example if an email newsletter with consent-based sign-up is introduced — this page will be updated first, before any data collection begins.

Questions about privacy: use the contact page.
