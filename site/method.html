Editorial method & verification policy

Where our numbers come from

  • Primary sources only for volatile facts. Prices, plan limits, commission rates, cookie windows and restrictions are taken from the vendor's own pages (pricing page, affiliate program page, legal terms) — never from third-party roundups.
  • Date stamps. Every volatile fact carries the date it was verified and a link to the source page. If we cannot verify a fact, we do not publish it.
  • Re-verification. All registry entries are re-checked at least quarterly (currently due 2026-12-03). Pages are updated with a new date when terms change.
  • Labelled claims. We separate: verified vendor data · our own testing in free plans/demos · calculations and models · assumptions. Models (like the calculator's earnings estimate) are assumptions, never forecasts of what you will earn.

How we test products

When we say we tested something, it means the publisher, William Robertsson, created an account in the vendor's free plan or demo and used the features described. We never claim to have bought, paid for or long-run a product unless documented on this page. Testing entries are listed with dates as they happen.

Corrections

Found an error? Contact us. Material corrections get a visible note on the affected page with the date of the change.

Affiliate policy

Some outbound links may be affiliate links. Affiliate status is always disclosed near the link, and affiliate links carry rel="sponsored". Commissions never influence our recommendations; where we rank platforms we state the criteria. Current registered programs and their verified terms are listed on the calculator page sources.
