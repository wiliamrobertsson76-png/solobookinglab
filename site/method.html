Method, test protocol & verification policy

The three labels

  • Verified by our own test — we ran the product through the protocol below in a real account and documented the date and what we saw. Nothing carries this label today: our own account tests have not started yet.
  • Verified in vendor documentation [vendor, date] — taken from the vendor's own pricing page, help center or program page, with the date we fetched it.
  • Not verified [unverified] — we do not know. We will not repeat vendor marketing as fact. Unverified points are listed so you know what to test in your trial.

Third-party facts are marked [third-party, date] and are always weaker than vendor or own-test labels. Where a vendor document and a third-party source disagree (e.g. which plan includes deposits), we show both.

Published product test protocol (v1, 2026-09-05)

Every scheduler is tested with the same workflow, in a free or trial account, before we claim anything from our own testing:

  1. Create a mobile service with a service area.
  2. Collect client address and job details at booking.
  3. Add a travel-time buffer.
  4. Require a deposit.
  5. Enable reminders.
  6. Test cancellation and rescheduling from the client side.
  7. Test recurring booking where the feature exists.
  8. Export client and booking data.
  9. Document mobile experience, limits and which paid plan each feature required.

Test entries appear on this page with dates as they are performed. Accounts for testing are created by the publisher with real details; we never publish fake experiences.

How the Fit Finder works

The Fit Finder is rule-based: your answers create a needs list, and each scheduler is checked against verified capabilities only. Capabilities we have not verified count as unknown and are shown as unverified in the result — they never silently improve or hurt a ranking beyond an explicit "not verified by us" note. The full data table with per-fact labels ships inside the page source (site/js/fit-finder.js) so every result is reproducible.

Affiliate policy

Some outbound links may be affiliate links, disclosed near where they appear and marked rel="sponsored". Commission never affects the rules, the labels, the protocol or the ranking: products without affiliate programs (today: Square Appointments, Fresha, Calendly) are included on merit under identical rules. We never present ordinary destination links as affiliate links, and we do not recommend a product because it pays more.

Fact freshness

Pricing and program terms are volatile. Every volatile fact carries its verification date. Entries are re-checked at least quarterly (next scheduled re-check: 2026-12-05). When terms change, pages are updated with new dates.
