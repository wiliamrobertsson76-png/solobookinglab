Corrections policy & log

How to report an error

Open a public issue at github.com/wiliamrobertsson76-png/solobookinglab/issues — or use the contact page. You do not need to be polite about it; you do need to say which claim and (if possible) what the correct information is.

What happens next

  1. We re-check the claim against its primary source.
  2. Material corrections get a visible, dated note on the affected page — not a silent edit.
  3. The correction is logged below so the history stays public.
