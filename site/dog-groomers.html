Booking software for mobile dog groomers

A grooming van is a moving appointment book: every client is at a different address, every visit needs travel time, and a single no-show wastes a slot you can never resell. Here is what your booking stack must do — and what we have (and have not) verified about the tools on the market.

What a mobile groomer should demand from a booking stack

  1. Client address + job details at booking. Dog count, size/breed, coat condition, gate access, parking for the van.
  2. Travel-time buffer between appointments — back-to-back bookings break when the van is the constraint.
  3. Deposits or card-held no-show protection. A missed grooming slot is pure lost revenue.
  4. Automated reminders (SMS if possible) — owners forget mobile visits more than fixed-location ones.
  5. Recurring bookings for the 6–8 week rebook pattern.
  6. Easy cancellation/rescheduling rules you control (cutoff windows, fees) without manual chasing.
  7. Data export — client and booking history must leave with you.
