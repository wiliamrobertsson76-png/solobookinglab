## Swedish / EU requirements (verified 2026-09-04 against general legal knowledge — re-verify against
primary sources before first commercial publication; treat this file as the register)
1. **Marketing identification:** all commercial content must be clearly identifiable as marketing;
   responsible sender (William Robertsson, Sweden) must be evident. (Marknadsföringsförordningen /
   UCPD-aligned rules; marknadsföring ska tydligt kunna identifieras.)
2. **Affiliate disclosure:** clear, understandable, BEFORE or immediately adjacent to the first
   commercial link — not only in the footer. Standing Swedish disclosure text is stored in
   site templates: *"Den här sidan innehåller affiliate-länkar. Om du köper via dem får vi en
   provision utan extra kostnad för dig. Utgivare: William Robertsson (enskild person), Sverige."*
   English pages use the equivalent English text.
3. **Claims:** any price/discount/feature/result statement must be current and verifiable; each
   volatile fact carries verified-date + source. Comparative advertising must be objective,
   relevant, checkable, non-disparaging.
4. **Cookies/consent:** non-essential cookies require prior consent, equal ease to refuse and
   withdraw. Mitigation: site ships ZERO cookies and zero third-party scripts; KPIs run on
   aggregate, non-identifying, self-hosted counters only.
5. **Data minimisation:** no personal data in repos, logs, analytics. Newsletter consent (if ever
   launched) requires documented opt-in, clear sender, one-click unsubscribe. No cold email.
6. **GDPR basics:** lawful basis, minimisation, no sensitive data; contact page shows the
   individual publisher; no fake "editorial team".
