# STRATEGY — NewsletterStack (v1, 2026-09-04)

## Chosen niche
**Helping newsletter creators choose, run and migrate between email/newsletter platforms** —
with a genuinely useful free tool set (cost & affiliate-earnings calculator, migration checklists,
verified pricing database). Audience: creators with 0–100k subscribers choosing a platform or
planning to monetize/switch.

## Why this niche (evidence, not vibes)
Scoring method: 10 candidates rated 0–10 on six weighted criteria
(köpavsikt/intäktspotential 30 %, mervärde/originalitet 25 %, konkurrens 15 %,
programtillgång+villkor 15 %, verifierbarhet 10 %, låg risk 5 %).
Scores are grounded in verified primary-source facts (affiliate terms + pricing pages, checked
2026-09-04) and honest proxies where no tool data was available (marked "proxy").

| Candidate | Weighted score | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Newsletter/email platform choice** | **8.4** | 4+ strong recurring programs verified; free tiers testable by us; real tool gaps; global audience; no YMYL |
| Email marketing general guides | 7.1 | Same programs but broader = more competition, less specific tool value |
| Notion/productivity templates | 6.5 | Notion affiliate closed to new affiliates (2026-09-04); template space saturated |
| Web hosting for beginners | 5.9 | One-time bounties (Hostinger ~40% initial purchase), brutal competition |
| VPN/privacy tools | 5.2 | One-time commissions, saturated, often requires trust we lack |
| AI writing tools | 5.8 | Recurring but plans expensive, churn risk, claims hard to verify |
| Project management SaaS | 6.0 | Good programs but "best PM tool" SERPs dominated by giants |
| Course platforms (Teachable/Thinkific) | 6.8 | 30% lifetime recurring verified (Thinkific), but audience needs course creation we can't demo deeply |
| Crypto/fintech | 1.8 | Excluded: YMYL, regulatory risk |
| Health/supplements | 1.5 | Excluded: YMYL, physical claims we cannot verify |

Key verified facts backing the winner (checked 2026-09-04, URLs in state/link-registry.json):
- Kit: 50% year-one commission on referred payments; Creator plan $33/mo (annual billing, 1k subs);
  **free plan up to 10,000 subscribers** — easy to demo and verify.
- beehiiv: 50% year one (Bronze) up to 60% (Gold); 60-day cookie; Launch free ≤2,500 subs;
  Scale $43/mo billed annually.
- MailerLite: 30% lifetime recurring; 45-day cookie; free ≤250 subs.
- Systeme.io: 60% lifetime recurring, lifetime attribution, no application needed (instant ID).

## Value proposition
1. **The calculator** (site/calculator): "What will this platform cost me at my list size, and what
   would I earn as an affiliate?" — all numbers carry verified-date + source. Competitor comparison
   posts embed marketing claims; we publish verifiable, dated data.
2. **Migration & switching guides** (Kit ↔ beehiiv ↔ MailerLite ↔ Substack): concrete steps,
   checklists, what breaks (automations, tags, paid subscriptions).
3. **Honest platform comparisons** for one concrete use case at a time (e.g. "first 1,000 subs",
   "paid newsletter under 5k subs").

## Audience & jobs-to-be-done
- Creator choosing first platform (job: decide fast, avoid migration pain later).
- Creator hitting free-tier limit (job: decide stay-or-switch with real numbers).
- Creator wanting to monetize recommendations (job: understand which programs pay what).

## Channels
1. Owned site (primary). 2. Search/AEO: comparison + calculator + migration intent.
3. Later, only with OAuth + channel rules respected: short-form posts pointing to tools.
No paid traffic. No brand-term bidding (prohibited by program terms anyway).

## Revenue model & expectations (assumptions, NOT forecasts)
Affiliate commissions only (verified programs above). All revenue numbers in reports are labeled
observed fact / platform data / calculation / assumption. No income is guaranteed; nothing is
reported as forecast dressed as outcome.

## Risks & mitigations
- Program terms change → quarterly re-verification of every registry entry; publish date-stamped.
- SERP competition → win on tool + data freshness + migration guides, not generic listicles.
- Single-channel dependence → tracked via KPI "program concentration" and "channel dependence".
- Swedish consumer/marketing law → docs/COMPLIANCE.md checked per publication.
